# Servd — feature & price summary

Compact reference for loading into an AI chatbot. Philippine Pesos.
Verified against live pricing 7 Sep 2026. Authority: Super-admin → Feature
pricing. If a customer's billing screen differs, the screen is right.

Servd is an all-in-one restaurant POS + online ordering system for the
Philippines. One-time payments only. No monthly fee. No commission on sales.

## Free — every account, never gated

QR dine-in ordering · counter/takeout QR with order numbers · 1 table QR ·
cashier POS · kitchen display · split payments, bills and tips · void/edit with
manager approval · dietary tags · mark items sold out from the till · thermal
receipt printing · auto kitchen printer (network, cloud or Bluetooth) ·
customer feedback + Google review prompts · 3 staff accounts

## ₱499 one-time — activate online ordering

Turns on the ordering website for pickup and delivery. A restaurant builds and
previews its page free, and pays only when ready to take real orders.

Each extra branch is also ₱499, one time. One login runs every branch.

## Payments

Servd takes no cut. Customers scan the restaurant's own GCash, Maya or bank
QR and attach the reference; the money goes straight to the restaurant. No
gateway, no gateway fee, no commission.
